KraftYarn — Craft Shop Web UI Design

A creative and user-friendly web UI design for a craft shop, created to showcase handmade products in an organized and visually appealing way. The design focuses on easy navigation, product presentation, and a warm, creative aesthetic that reflects the shop’s handmade concept.

A front-end game website inspired by popular online gaming platforms, designed using HTML and CSS. It features a simple and organized layout where users can browse different games and select a game they want to play. Each game includes a link that directs players to the actual game website.

My IT Journey

My journey as an IT student, from my first year until my third year.

1st Year

My First Year

My first year introduced me to programming and the basic concepts of Information Technology. At first, I had little to no knowledge about coding, but as I learned more, I became interested in how programming can be used to create solutions and help advance technology. I also met people who share the same interests and goals, which made my journey more enjoyable and motivated me to keep learning and exploring the world of IT.

2nd Year

My Second Year

My second year helped me improve my programming skills and understand different areas of IT. The lessons became broader and more challenging, which pushed me to step outside my comfort zone. With the support and encouragement of my friends, I continued to learn, faced the challenges, and stayed strong throughout the journey.

3rd Year

My Third Year

My third year marks another important step in my journey as an IT student. I’m taking on bigger projects, learning more advanced concepts, and exploring different areas of technology. I know there will be more challenges ahead, but I see them as opportunities to learn, improve, and become more confident in my abilities. With every project and experience, I continue to gain knowledge, develop my skills, and prepare myself for the career I want to pursue in the future.
